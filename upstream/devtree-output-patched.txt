What to look for: on Windows the grandparent is the USB composite
device USB\VID_2B73&PID_0026\<serial> and its children are MI_00..MI_04,
one of which is the MIDI interface. If the grandparent is ROOT\WINE\WINEBUS
and the siblings are unrelated devices, the tree cannot express this
hardware and no amount of correct API will find the MIDI interface.
