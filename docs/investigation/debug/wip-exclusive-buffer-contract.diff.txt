WORK IN PROGRESS — exclusive-mode buffer contract. NOT shipping.

Against dlls/winealsa.drv/alsa.c as of the 0003 shipping patch. Contains three
substantive changes plus the debug probes that measured them:

  1. alsa_get_render_buffer judges free space by what is still in the mmdevapi
     ring (data_not_in_alsa) rather than held_frames, which counts frames Wine
     has already copied into ALSA.
  2. The ring is allocated deeper than the buffer advertised to the client
     (mmdev_bufsize_frames), so a full advertised buffer can actually be freed.
  3. For EXCLUSIVE event-driven streams the advertised buffer is one period,
     which is the contract Windows implements.

Measured: AUDCLNT_E_BUFFER_TOO_LARGE went from ~158,000/second to zero,
GetBuffer began succeeding ~385 times a second, and the audio actually reaching
the DDJ went from 3.0 s in 45 s to 15 s in 40 s.

NOT shipped because the stream is still rebuilt every ~7-8 s (no Wine-side error
anywhere in the lifecycle) and because the single-device case — PC MASTER OUT
off, which is how the machine is used day to day — has NOT been re-measured
against it. Advertising 256 frames where the client asked for 1024 is a real
semantic change and could regress that path.

Debug probes included: RBW-TOOBIG (the refusal, with the numbers that decide
it), RBW-GETOK (successes — a falling failure rate is not the same as a client
being served), RBW-POS (reported play position; established that rekordbox never
calls GetPosition at all, so the clock is NOT the rebuild trigger).
